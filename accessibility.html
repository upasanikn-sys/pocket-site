Accessibility

Checked before every publish · Written 29 October 2026 · Applies to this website
In short: this website aims to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA. Before any change is published, Pocket's own test tools check every page, in light and dark and at phone and desktop sizes, against the standards below, and nothing ships while one fails. The checks are automated. Nobody who uses a screen reader every day has tested the site yet; that is the next step, and the limits below say what is not yet known.

On this page

  1. What every page is held to
  2. What is not yet known
  3. Tell us what got in the way
  4. Version history

1. What every page is held to

  • Contrast. Text stands at least 4.5:1 against what is behind it, and at least 5:1 when it is 14px or smaller. No text is smaller than 12px, in a drawing either.
  • Room to press. Every button and control is at least 24 by 24 pixels; a link inside a sentence takes the size of its text.
  • Keyboard. Each page starts with a link that skips to its content. On the inner pages a keyboard walk reaches every link and control with Tab and finds a visible ring on each.
  • Names. Every control has a name a screen reader can read out, and on the inner pages every figure does too.
  • Zoom and narrow screens. No page scrolls sideways at any width from 320 to 1728 pixels, in light or dark.
  • High contrast. With Windows' contrast themes on, no mark drawn in colour disappears.
  • Motion. With Reduce motion on, nothing animates: every change happens at once and every figure shows without a transition.
  • Automated rules. axe-core finds no serious or critical problem on any page.
  • Film. The product film has captions, and it plays only when you press play.

2. What is not yet known

  • No one who relies on NVDA, VoiceOver or TalkBack has walked the site yet, and no full screen-reader pass has been recorded. Automated checks catch much, not all.
  • Try the sample shows the app's own screens, which keep the app's floor of 4.5:1 for all text rather than this site's 5:1 for small text.
  • This page is about the website. The Pocket app has checks of its own, and its accessibility is not described here.

3. Tell us what got in the way

Email support@mypocketfi.com with the page and what stopped you, and the setup you used if you can (browser, screen reader, zoom). Every email is read and answered by the founder.
