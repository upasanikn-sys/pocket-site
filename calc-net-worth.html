How it adds up

  • Investments at today's value, not at what they cost.
  • Accounts are everything else you own: bank balances and fixed deposits, EPF, PPF and NPS, and gold, property or a vehicle at a value you choose.
  • Liabilities at what is still owed: a loan's outstanding balance, a card's balance today.
  • Each thing counts once. A mutual fund belongs under investments, so it is not an account as well. A home with its home loan counts both: the home under accounts and the loan under liabilities.
  • Every amount is added in paise, so the working adds up to the figure exactly.
