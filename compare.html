No ranking and no named products: each category is for something different.

A spreadsheet: yours to build, yours to get right.

Pocket imports and adds up for you, and shows each sum.

QuestionA spreadsheetPocket
Where the data livesIn the file you save, wherever you keep itIn a database on your computer or phone. No server, no account
Who does the sumsYou, in formulas you write and keep rightPocket, the same way every time; Show working lists the entries behind a figure
StatementsYou paste or reformat themBank CSVs and CAMS or KFintech statements, imported only where every line adds up
India's taxOnly if you build the slabsA planning estimate on the Finance Act's slabs, rebate and surcharge, not a filing

Account-aggregator apps: your accounts, through a regulated route.

Pocket takes the other route: a file you import, and no bank login.

QuestionAn aggregator appPocket
How it reaches your accountsThrough an account aggregator, with your explicit consentA statement file you download yourself and import. No bank login
What it can seeWhat the institutions share through that consentWhat is in the file you choose to import
Where the figures are keptVaries by app: read its privacy policyOn your device. Four connections exist, each off until you turn it on

In India an account aggregator must be registered with the Reserve Bank and works on the customer's explicit consent: RBI Master Direction, Account Aggregator (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2016.

A broker's app: your holdings with that broker.

Pocket puts holdings from every place beside your accounts and debts.

QuestionA broker's appPocket

Your other moneyHeld elsewhere, so not in itCounted: cash, retirement accounts and loans sit beside the holdings
What it doesCan place orders, as a broker's app is forReports figures, targets and gaps. It never moves money or places a trade
