Support & Contact

Every email is read and answered by the founder
Email support@mypocketfi.com. Each button below opens your email app with the subject filled in; nothing is sent until you send it. There is no form, no chat widget and no account.

Download and install

The current Windows build (v3.2.10, 49 MB) and the Mac, Linux and Android builds are on the Download page, each with its SHA-256 and install help. Stuck? Say which device and what you saw.

Email about installing

Billing and refunds

A question about a purchase, or a refund within 30 days of any charge, with no reason needed. Write from your purchase email, or include the order number from your Gumroad receipt. The Refund Policy has the details.

Email for a refund

Licence recovery

Lost your key, or setting Pocket up on another device? Write from the address you bought with: that mailbox is the proof, and the key you were sent comes again. One key covers up to 3 devices that you use yourself.

Email for your key

Bugs

Something wrong, confusing or missing. The version (the file you downloaded is named with it), your device, what you did and what you saw; a screenshot of the sample data, not your own, if one helps. Check the known issues first.

Email a bug report

Security reports

A vulnerability goes to the same address with the subject SECURITY, which is read first. The disclosure process is section 11 of the security architecture; the machine-readable contact is security.txt.

Email a security report

Known issues

The current release's known limits, from its release note; every version's are on the Changelog, and new releases arrive in the release feed (RSS).

  • Pocket's tax figures are a planning estimate for salary income, not a tax return or tax advice. A premium paid in cash does not qualify for 80D, and one paid for several years at once is not split across them; Pocket assumes neither applies.
  • Not yet code-signed: Windows SmartScreen asks you to confirm the first run. The Mac build is not yet notarised by Apple. The Linux build runs in a terminal window.
  • Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store are coming.

Before you email

  • Where is my data? In the FinOSData folder on your own device: %USERPROFILE%\FinOSData on Windows, ~/FinOSData on a Mac or Linux. Back up that folder and you have everything.
  • Windows or macOS stops the first open? The builds are not yet code-signed or notarised. On Windows choose "More info", then "Run anyway"; on a Mac, System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. Check the file against its SHA-256 on the Download page first.
  • The trial ended? Nothing is lost: Pocket becomes the Free plan, and a licence key brings the Pro features back.
  • Is there a newer version? Settings, Privacy & security, Check now, or the Changelog.
