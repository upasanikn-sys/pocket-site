Old vs new tax regime, worked through

Category
Tax · FY 2026-27
By
Pocket
Reviewed
12 Oct 2026
Read
5 min
In short: the new regime has lower rates and a larger rebate but almost no deductions; the old regime has higher rates but lets you deduct 80C, 80D, HRA and more. Which costs less depends on how much you can deduct. On a salary of ₹15,00,000, the old regime costs less only once its deductions pass about ₹5,43,750. Budget 2026 left the slabs, the rebate and the surcharge as they were for FY 2025-26.

The two regimes for FY 2026-27

Each regime taxes income in bands (slabs). These are the rates for a resident individual under 60:

Taxable incomeRate

  • Standard deduction from a salary: ₹75,000 in the new regime, ₹50,000 in the old.
  • Rebate: no tax at all on taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 in the new regime, and up to ₹5,00,000 in the old. That is section 87A of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and section 156 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, which applies from April 2026. With the standard deduction, a salary of up to ₹12,75,000 pays no tax in the new regime.
  • Cess: 4% of the tax, in both.
  • Surcharge on incomes above ₹50 lakh: 10%, 15% above ₹1 crore and 25% above ₹2 crore in both. Above ₹5 crore it is 37% in the old regime and still 25% in the new.

Just above ₹12 lakh: marginal relief

Without relief, earning one rupee over the rebate limit would cost tens of thousands in tax. Marginal relief limits the tax to the income above ₹12,00,000. On a salary of ₹13,00,000:

  • taxable income: ₹13,00,000 − ₹75,000 = ₹12,25,000;
  • tax on the slabs: ₹63,750;
  • income above ₹12,00,000: ₹25,000, so the tax is limited to ₹25,000;
  • with 4% cess: ₹26,000 for the year.

Relief stops mattering once the slab tax is lower than the income above the limit, from a salary of ₹13,45,589.

How much would the old regime need?

For each salary below, the deductions the old regime would need, beyond its standard deduction, to cost no more than the new regime.

Deductions include 80C (at most ₹1,50,000), 80D, the HRA exemption, professional tax and the others the old regime allows.

Questions

Is there any tax on a salary of ₹12,75,000 in the new regime?

No. After the standard deduction of ₹75,000 the taxable income is ₹12,00,000, and the rebate leaves no tax on taxable income up to that.

What is marginal relief?

Just above the rebate limit the new regime's tax is at most the income above ₹12,00,000. On a salary of ₹13,00,000 that is ₹26,000 for the year, with cess.

Which regime costs less on a salary of ₹18,00,000?

With the full 80C of ₹1,50,000 and an 80D of ₹25,000, the new regime is ₹1,45,600 lower: ₹1,50,800 against ₹2,96,400.
