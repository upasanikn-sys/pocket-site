What Pocket will never do.

Trust needs boundaries as much as it needs features.

  • No trades.It never executes transactions or moves real money. Planning only.
  • No bank logins.It never asks for credentials. Imports are file-based, by design.
  • No guarantees.Projections are illustrations with stated assumptions, never promised outcomes.
  • No advice.Not a SEBI-registered adviser, and not a replacement for one.
  • No data grab.Your financial database is never uploaded to Pocket servers by default.

Is Pocket free?

Yes. The Free plan tracks everything, with no time limit and no card. Each install starts with 14 days of Founder Pro; after that Pocket stays on Free unless you buy Founder Pro (₹499 a month or ₹4,999 a year) or Lifetime Founder (₹14,999 once).

Does Pocket need my bank login?

No. It reads the statements you download from your bank and your mutual-fund registrar, on your device, and never asks for a login, a password to your bank or an OTP.

Where is my data kept?

On your own device: in a database on your computer, which your phone can open over your own Wi-Fi, or inside the Android app. There is no Pocket server and no account, so there is nothing of yours for Pocket to see.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), macOS on Apple silicon, Linux (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13 or newer, and an AppImage), and Android 7.0 or later. Pocket 3.2.10, published 12 October 2026.

Is Pocket investment advice?

No. Pocket is not registered with SEBI and gives no advice. It reports each figure, its target and the gap between them, and leaves the decision with you.
