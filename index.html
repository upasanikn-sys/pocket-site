What it reads.

Files you already have, and a few things typed once. Nothing is fetched from your bank.

A bank statementCSV
From any bank: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, Axis Bank and Kotak are read by name, any other by its date, description and amount columns. What went out becomes spending, what came in income.
A mutual-fund statementPDF
The detailed CAS that CAMS or KFintech emails you, opened with its password, which is never stored. A fund is imported only when every line adds up to the statement’s closing units and value.
Your investmentsCSV
One row per entry: date, symbol, type (buy, SIP, sell, dividend, interest, fee, deposit or withdrawal), quantity and price, with fees and a dollar rate where they apply.
What no file holdstyped
Your payslip, accounts, loans, policies, bills and goals, each typed once into a labelled form.

Each is read on this device. A backup is made before every import, rows Pocket already has are skipped, and a row it cannot read is named, never guessed. It never asks for a bank or broker login.

Your financial data never leaves your device.

Not by default, and never quietly. There is no Pocket account and no Pocket server. Live prices, encrypted sync and the update check happen only if you switch them on, and go only where you send them. Open each to see what it would send.

What Pocket will never do.

Trust needs boundaries as much as it needs features.

  • No trades.It never executes transactions or moves real money. Planning only.
  • No bank logins.It never asks for credentials. Imports are file-based, by design.
  • No guarantees.Projections are illustrations with stated assumptions, never promised outcomes.
  • No advice.Not a SEBI-registered adviser, and not a replacement for one.
  • No data grab.Your financial database is never uploaded to Pocket servers by default.

Asked first.

Plain answers, with the figures. The rest are in the guides and on Support.

Is Pocket free?

Yes. The Free plan tracks everything, with no time limit and no card. Each install starts with 14 days of Founder Pro; after that Pocket stays on Free unless you buy Founder Pro (₹499 a month or ₹4,999 a year) or Lifetime Founder (₹14,999 once).

Does Pocket need my bank login?

No. It reads the statements you download from your bank and your mutual-fund registrar, on your device, and never asks for a login, a password to your bank or an OTP.

Where is my data kept?

On your own device: in a database on your computer, which your phone can open over your own Wi-Fi, or inside the Android app. There is no Pocket server and no account, so there is nothing of yours for Pocket to see.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), macOS on Apple silicon, Linux on x86-64 (a .deb for glibc 2.38 or newer, and an AppImage), and Android 7.0 or later. Pocket 3.2.10, published 12 October 2026.

Is Pocket investment advice?

No. Pocket is not registered with SEBI and gives no advice. It reports each figure, its target and the gap between them, and leaves the decision with you.
