Your financial data never leaves your device.

Not by default, and never quietly. There is no Pocket account and no Pocket server. Live prices, encrypted sync and hosted AI happen only if you switch them on, and go only where you send them.

  • Live pricesOffInstrument identifiers such as ticker symbols, never your name, quantities or account details.
  • Encrypted syncOffAn encrypted copy, sealed with your passphrase, goes only to the folder you choose.
  • Hosted AIOffOnly with your own API key: a numeric summary such as totals and allocation, never names, notes or entries.

What Pocket will never do.

Trust needs boundaries as much as it needs features.

  • No trades.It never executes transactions or moves real money. Planning only.
  • No bank logins.It never asks for credentials. Imports are file-based, by design.
  • No guarantees.Projections are illustrations with stated assumptions, never promised outcomes.
  • No advice.Not a SEBI-registered adviser, and not a replacement for one.
  • No data grab.Your financial database is never uploaded to Pocket servers by default.
