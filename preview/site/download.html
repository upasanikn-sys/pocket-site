What happens when you request access

  1. You email us — no payment, no form, no account.
  2. A reply from us (check spam) delivers the zip, normally within 48 hours. Our address is changing; the new one will be on the Contact page.
  3. Unzip anywhere and run Pocket.exe. Your 14-day full trial starts on first run, then the Free plan continues forever.
  4. Data lives in %USERPROFILE%\FinOSData — back up that folder and you have everything. Uninstall = delete the app folder.
  5. Updates arrive by email with release notes; back up before updating. A public changelog page is planned.
