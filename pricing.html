Questions about paying

What does the trial include?

Every new install runs as Founder Pro for 14 days, with no card. Then it is Free, with your entries still there.

What happens if I stop paying?

Pocket becomes Free. Reading, exporting and deleting your data never need a plan, and the vault stays readable.

How does my key arrive, and how many devices does it cover?

Your key comes by email after you pay. Paste it into Settings; it is checked on the device. One key covers 3 of your devices.

How do refunds work?

Email support@mypocketfi.com within 30 days of any charge, with your order number; no reason needed. The money goes back to how you paid, and Pocket returns to Free with your data intact. Refund Policy.

How do I cancel Founder Pro?

On Gumroad, or by email to support@mypocketfi.com. Pro runs to the end of the period paid.

Which currency, and how do I pay?

Rupees, paid at pocketfinance.gumroad.com. Pocket never sees your card; Gumroad never sees your figures.
