Frequently asked questions

Do you store my data on any servers?

No. Pocket is local-first — your data lives only on your device, in a local SQLite database. There is no Pocket server, no account, and nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly back up to your own cloud folder. The only other connections, live market prices and hosted AI, stay off until you switch them on. (The document vault and backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM; full database-at-rest encryption is on our roadmap.)

Can I use Pocket completely offline?

Yes — everything core works offline. An internet connection is only used — optionally, once you switch them on — to refresh live market prices from free public feeds, or for hosted AI.

Will my data be safe if I change my device?

Yes. On any plan, copy your data folder (or export CSV) to the new device. Founder Pro adds an encrypted backup you can restore there, through a cloud folder you choose (Pocket runs no server and holds no key). You always hold the keys — Pocket never does.

Can I cancel or switch plans anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel or change plans whenever you like. If you cancel a paid plan you simply drop to the Free plan — still fully usable forever, with all your data readable and exportable.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes — you get a full Pro trial to explore every premium feature. After it ends you continue on the generous Free plan for as long as you like, and can upgrade anytime.

How do paid plans work without an account?

Your financial data never needs a login — it lives only on your device. When you buy a plan, we email you a signed license key; enter it once and it activates offline, forever. The only thing we ever hold is the purchase email used to send your key. Changing devices? Just re-enter your key.

Does Pocket connect to my bank?

No — by design. You import CSV statements (one-click presets for HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis and Kotak, plus auto-detect) or add transactions yourself. No credentials are ever stored, so nothing can leak. Automatic bank connections are not supported.

What is the refund policy?

30 days, no questions asked, on paid plans. Email us at the address on the Contact page with your order id and the refund goes back to your original payment method — full details in the refund policy. Your data stays on your device either way — the app simply returns to the Free plan.

How do I get Pocket?

Download it for Windows from the Download page — no payment, no form, no account. Your 14-day full trial starts on first run; afterwards the Free plan continues forever, and a paid licence key (delivered by email after purchase) unlocks premium features offline.
