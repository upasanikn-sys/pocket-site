Questions about paying

What does the trial include?

Every new install runs as Founder Pro for its first 14 days, with no card and no sign-up. Then it is the Free plan, with everything you entered still there.

What happens if I stop paying?

Pocket becomes the Free plan. Reading, exporting, backing up and deleting your own data are never behind a plan, and the documents already in the vault stay readable.

How does my key arrive, and how many devices does it cover?

After you pay on Gumroad, your licence key comes by email. Paste it into Settings (on the phone, Plans & pricing); it is checked on the device, with no account and no network call. One key covers up to 3 devices that you use yourself.

How do refunds work?

Email support@mypocketfi.com within 30 days of any charge, with your order number; no reason is needed. The money goes back through Gumroad to how you paid, and Pocket returns to the Free plan with your data as it was. The Refund Policy has the details.

How do I cancel Founder Pro?

On Gumroad, or by email to support@mypocketfi.com. Pro runs to the end of the period you paid for, then Pocket is the Free plan.

Which currency, and how do I pay?

Prices are in rupees. Gumroad takes the payment at pocketfinance.gumroad.com and sends your receipt. Pocket never sees your card, and Gumroad never sees your figures.
