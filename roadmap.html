How this roadmap is decided

One founder reads every vote
A vote is an email to support@mypocketfi.com with the subject filled in. No account, no public board.
No dates until it ships
Next and Later have no dates. A version ships when its checks pass; the Changelog says when.
Off until you turn it on
Anything that sends data off your device ships switched off, and is named on the Security page first.
