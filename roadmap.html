  2. Now

    Built, waiting on a store or a certificate

    In progress

    The stores, and signed builds

    The release notes list these under Known limits. The code is done; each waits on an outside account or review.

    • Google PlayThe Android app as a store install, with a build made for the store since 3.2.4. Until the listing is live, the Android app is an APK on the Download page.
    • The App StoreThe phone app on iPhone. There is no iPhone app to download until the listing is live.
    • Microsoft StoreThe Windows app as a Store install, updated by the Store. Until then, the installer on the Download page.
    • Signed Windows and notarised Mac buildsUntil then, Windows SmartScreen and macOS ask you to confirm the first open; the Download page says how.

How this roadmap is decided

One founder reads every vote
A vote is an email to support@mypocketfi.com with the subject already filled in. There is no account and no public board.
No dates until it ships
Next and Later carry no dates. A version ships when its checks pass, and the Changelog says when.
Off until you turn it on
Anything that would send data off your device (live prices, the update check, sync, answers from Claude) ships switched off, and is named on the Security page first.
